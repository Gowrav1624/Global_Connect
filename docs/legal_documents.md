# Legal Documents

## 1. Purpose
This document identifies the legal and contractual documents relevant to the Global_Connect project.

## 2. Documents

### Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
An NDA may be used where confidential project information, credentials, business information, or source code must be protected between parties.

### Intellectual Property Agreement
The parties should define ownership and permitted use of source code, designs, documentation, data, and other project deliverables.

### Licensing
Third-party libraries and services used by the project must be used according to their applicable licenses and terms.

### Support and Maintenance Agreement
Support responsibilities, support period, response expectations, maintenance scope, and exclusions should be documented between the relevant parties.

## 3. Project Responsibilities
The project team should:
- Avoid redistributing third-party material without permission.
- Maintain records of applicable open-source licenses.
- Protect confidential credentials and information.
- Obtain required client approvals for deliverables.

## 4. Privacy
The application should handle user data according to the approved privacy requirements and applicable laws.

## 5. Legal Review
This document is a project documentation template and does not replace legal advice. Actual contracts should be reviewed and executed by the authorized parties.
